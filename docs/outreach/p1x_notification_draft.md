**Тема (англ.):** Heads-up: open-source Solana fork of Tanks of Freedom II

Hi P1X team,

I'm writing as a courtesy heads-up. We are building an open-source fork of
Tanks of Freedom II (your MIT-licensed repository) that reworks the game into
a Solana-based turn-based tactics dApp for the Solana Mobile dApp Store.

A few things we want to be explicit about:

1. We fully respect your MIT license: the original copyright notice will be
   preserved in an in-game "Open Source Credits" screen and in our
   THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES file.
2. We are doing a complete rebrand: the name "Tanks of Freedom", your faction
   and character names, logo, and all links to your pages will be removed from
   our product. Our version will not present itself as a P1X game or imply any
   affiliation with you.
3. We are replacing the audio entirely, so no CC BY-SA obligations carry over.
   Your devlog media in `docs/` is not used or redistributed.
4. Our additions are on-chain matchmaking, wallet connect, and a royalty layer
   for community map authors (built on top of your excellent map editor).

We'd be glad to hear if you have any concerns, and we're open to collaboration
or a proper attribution arrangement if you'd prefer one. Thank you for
releasing such a great game under a permissive license.
